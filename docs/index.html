Crafting Work Orders

Post custom crafting and enchanting requests with material toggles (Mats Provided vs No Mats), quantity, and notes. Crafters claim orders to prevent duplicate work.

Share surplus ores, herbs, cloth, gems, and consumables directly from your bags. Browse the virtual guild stockpile and request materials with 1 click.

Complete Vanilla (1–300) and TBC (300–375) encyclopedic database for Mining, Herbalism, Skinning, Elements/Primals, Cloth, and Fishing with optimal farming locations.

Supply Bounties & Postal Handshake

Movable onscreen overlay with visual progress bars and live bag loot auto-counters. Pin items from the Resource Atlas or active Guild Bounties with 1 click.

Slash Commands Reference

Quick in-game commands to access any view instantly.

Command Description
/gsf Toggle the main GSFHub interface window.
/gsf settings Open the dedicated Settings & Configuration window.
/gsf atlas Open directly to the 1–375 Resource Farming Atlas & Bounties tab.
/gsf hud Toggle the onscreen Personal Goals HUD overlay.
/gsf scan Manually trigger a profession scan and broadcast updates to the guild.
/gsf bug Open the in-game Diagnostic & Bug Report copy modal.
/gsf help Display all available slash commands in chat.

Guild Leader & Officer Quick Start

Getting your guild set up with GSFHub takes less than 2 minutes.

1

Install the Addon

Download and extract the GSFHub folder directly into your World of Warcraft/_classic_/Interface/AddOns/ directory.

2

Open Trade Skills Once

Open your character's profession windows (e.g. Tailoring, Alchemy, Blacksmithing). GSFHub silently indexes all known recipes and assigns your guild role badges ([✨ Master Crafter], [🌿 Herbalist]).

3

Automatic Peer-to-Peer Mesh

No server or database setup required! As guild members log in, GSFHub exchanges differential compressed packets automatically over the hidden guild communication channel.
